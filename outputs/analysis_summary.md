# 2024 Bahrain Grand Prix — VER vs PER

## Result

The distance-aligned telemetry gives a final delta of **1.589 s** in favour of **VER**.
The selected laps were lap 39 (SOFT) for VER and lap 40 (SOFT) for PER.

## Quantitative observations

- Maximum speeds: VER 301.0 km/h; PER 302.8 km/h.
- Mean speeds: VER 228.8 km/h; PER 225.6 km/h.
- Full-throttle distance: VER 62.3%; PER 58.5%.
- Braking distance: VER 16.5%; PER 19.9%.

## Interpretation and limitations

The plot shows where the lap-time gap grows, but it does not by itself prove why it grows. Fuel load, tyre condition, traffic, energy deployment and telemetry sampling can all affect the comparison. Using same-team drivers reduces car-performance differences, but it does not remove these limitations.
